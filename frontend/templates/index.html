Food Safety Practices

Keep Your Kitchen Safe

Simple, reliable food safety habits that help prevent food waste and illness.

🧊 Temperature Control

Keep your refrigerator at or below 40°F (4°C). Perishable foods left above 40°F for over two hours enter the danger zone for rapid bacterial growth.

👃 Smell & Texture Inspection

Never taste food that looks, smells, or feels suspicious. Spoilage bacteria and molds can be harmful even if discoloration is not yet widespread.

🌿 Ethylene Isolation

Keep high-ethylene producers like apples and bananas separated from ethylene-sensitive leafy greens, carrots, and potatoes to delay overripening.
